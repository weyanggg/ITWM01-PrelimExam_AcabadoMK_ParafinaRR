About Us

Getting to know SMILEIH Dental Clinic

Company Overview

Our Story

SMILEIH Dental Clinic was founded on the belief that everyone deserves access to comfortable, high-quality dental care. What started as a single small clinic has grown into a trusted neighborhood practice serving hundreds of families.

Our team combines years of clinical experience with a genuine warmth that puts patients at ease from the moment they walk in, offering everything from routine cleanings to complete smile transformations.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional care, safe, and patient-centered dental care through advanced technology and continuous teamwork.

We are dedicated to providing a comfortable environment and that guides every patient toward a healthier, more confident smile.

Our Vision

To a be trusted and leading dental clinic in the community, recognized for clinical excellence, compassionate service, and lasting relationships.

We strive to create an environment where individuals and families feel safe, valued, and confident in their oral health and smiles.

Meet the Owner

Dr. Romeo Ritchie Parafina

Founder & Lead Dentist

With over 15 years of clinical experience, Dr. Romeo Ritchie Parafina founded SMILEIH Dental Clinic to give his community a warmer, more personal dental experience. He continues to see patients while mentoring the clinic's growing team.
